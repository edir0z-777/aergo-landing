Owner

Anat Rozenstein is the owner of AERGO LLC. Anat is responsible for the company and directs its client work in software development, technology consulting, and security architecture.

How the business operates

AERGO LLC sells professional services to other companies. A client engagement is agreed directly with that organization, delivered by AERGO LLC, and invoiced to the client. Work is performed remotely for business clients in the United States and abroad.

The company does not operate a consumer product or a marketplace, and it does not hold or transmit customer funds. Its work is engineering and advisory services in three areas.

  1. Software development

    Design and delivery of robust, scalable enterprise applications for client companies.

  2. Technology consulting

    Advisory work on technical roadmaps, systems design, and operations.

  3. Security architecture and audits

    Application security reviews, threat modeling, and infrastructure compliance verification.
